# IndianPol 2.0 Website

Premium responsive static website for IndianPol — a political organization platform for candidates, campaign teams and political leaders from Panchayat to Parliament.

## Founder content currently included

- **Aditya Yadav** — Founder, Managing Partner and CEO. Co-founder of Seetaara; received ₹15 lakh in seed funding from Startup India.
- **Deepak Pandey** — Co-Founder and Head of Sales & Operations. Founder of Santori Solar Solution, which raised ₹3 crore.
- **Deepak Mishra** — Co-Founder and Head of Technology & Digital Marketing. Founder of Seetaara.

Founder portrait blocks are intentionally photo-ready. Replace each `.portrait-placeholder` inside the corresponding `.founder-photo` with the real founder image when available.

## Replace work showcase

Replace the three `.work-placeholder` blocks in `index.html` with real project images and descriptions when available.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` directly, or serve the folder with any static server.

No build step is required.
